### 🛡️ 1.4. Critical Vulkan AMD Windows Rule
* **`--diffusion-fa` (Flash Attention) prohibited**: triggers the `split_k_reduce` / `ErrorDeviceLost` error under AMD driver 26.8.1.
* **Hybrid backend mandatory**: `--backend "diffusion=vulkan0,te=cpu,vae=cpu"` for models with multimodal encoders (LTX-2.5, MiniMax-H3) to avoid Vulkan buffer overflows.

### ⚡ 1.5. Video Sharpness Improvement: Lever 1 (AMD FidelityFX CAS)
* **Problem solved**: the stretching blur caused by the Lanczos filter alone when going from 480p/512p to 1080p.
* **Validated solution**: chaining the official **AMD Contrast Adaptive Sharpening** filter (`scale=1920:1080:flags=lanczos,cas=0.75`).
* **Benefit**: micro-contrast and edge separation (scales, fangs, pupils) instantly decupled (0 s of extra compute via `h264_amf` hardware encoding).
* **Dedicated script**: `scripts/test_levier1_cas.py` generating the CAS masters, the 50/50 split-screen comparison videos and the 1:1 zoom sheets.

### 🚀 1.6. Video Sharpness Improvement: Lever 4 (Real-ESRGAN 4K AI Super-Resolution)
* **Principle**: frame-by-frame extraction, passing through the `4x-UltraSharp.pth` neural network via `sd-cli -M upscale --backend vulkan0` (~7.3 s/frame on RX 6950 XT), then AMF hardware reassembly in Ultra HD 4K (3840×2160 @ 50 Mbps) with AMD FidelityFX CAS 0.75 filter.
* **Visual result**: **reconstruction of real micro-details** (grid lines, edges, specular, 3D textures) impossible to obtain by classic interpolation.

### 📺 1.7. YouTube Delivery Strategy & Maximum Sharpness
* **AVC1 compression bypass**: YouTube automatically applies a destructive bitrate (~4-6 Mbps) to 1080p videos.
* **Mandatory 4K Mastering**: uploading in **4K UHD (3840×2160)** forces YouTube to encode in **VP09 / AV01 at 30-45 Mbps**, guaranteeing surgical sharpness even on Full HD 1080p screens.
* **Master Conform Settings**: AMF bitrate 50 Mbps, AMD FidelityFX CAS 0.75, High 4:2:0 profile.

### ⏱️ 1.8. Production Standard: 5.5-second Scenes (165 frames at 30 FPS)
* **Target cadence**: 5.5 seconds = exactly 165 frames at 30 FPS.
* **Recommended Architecture: Multi-Shot I2V Chaining**:
  * Generation of 2 successive shots (~82 frames) or 3 shots (~55 frames).
  * Re-injecting the ultimate frame of shot N (`extraire_derniere_trame`) as the conditional seed (`-i`) of shot N+1.
  * Keeps VRAM < 11 GB, avoids the attentional O(N²) explosion (which saturates the 16 GB at 161 frames in a single block), and eliminates the temporal softening of long passes.
